Our story

Saffron from home

“Kesar” is saffron, “rasoi” the home kitchen. Meera and Arjun came to Turin from Lucknow in 2014 and opened Kesar Rasoi a year later, with Meera’s grandmother’s recipes and a hand-built tandoor.

Awadhi cooking is slow: the dal makhani simmers overnight, the biryani is sealed with dough and finishes in its own steam. We toast and grind our spices ourselves, every morning.
